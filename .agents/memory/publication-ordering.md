---
name: Local publication ordering
description: Meaning of publication time and honest ordering for older browser-local offers.
---

Treat publication time as first publication, not the latest reopening or edit. Do not reconstruct missing older sample history from modification times or the current clock. For equal pickup times, sort known first-publication times newest first, then unknown histories by stable sample ID.

**Why:** The local discovery requirement is stable ordering after edits. Older stored samples never recorded publication history, so backfilling would make an unsupported historical claim.

**How to apply:** Preserve known and unknown publication history through edits, closure, reopening and reload. New published fixtures may use their current sample creation time, explicitly described as such. Explain the unknown-history fallback when older samples appear in discovery.
