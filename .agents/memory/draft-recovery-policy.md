---
name: Browser-local draft recovery policy
description: Founder-approved scope, retention, and deletion behavior for fictional offer and application recovery.
---

Opt-in is per offer or application draft. Keep recovery copies in this browser for up to seven days, then delete them on save/submission, explicit discard, sample reset, or expiry. Restore only refills the form; it must not publish or submit, and same-day preparation confirmation must never be recovered as consent.

**Why:** The founder explicitly approved this opt-in and deletion policy for the prototype.

**How to apply:** Keep recovery data separate from saved prototype records and scoped to the simulated cook identity and meal ID. Only use fictional demo values; do not introduce accounts, backend storage, service provisioning, or real contact/personal data without a new decision.
